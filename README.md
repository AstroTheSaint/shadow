# Shadow Repository

This is a personal repository for deep self-reflection, life documentation, and personal growth tracking. It serves as a digital mirror of my inner world, capturing both where I am and where I aspire to be.

## Structure

- `personal/` - Basic personal information and biographical data
- `reflections/` - Deep personal reflections and self-analysis
  - `insecurities/` - Documentation of personal insecurities and growth areas
  - `aspirations/` - Future goals and deepest desires
  - `current-state/` - Present life situation and feelings
- `journal/` - Ongoing personal journal entries
- `growth/` - Personal development tracking and milestones

## Purpose

This repository serves as a structured way to document my personal journey, inner thoughts, and life details. It's designed to be a comprehensive self-reflection tool that helps track personal growth and maintain awareness of my authentic self.

## Usage

Regular updates and reflections should be added to maintain this as a living document of personal growth and self-awareness. # shadow
