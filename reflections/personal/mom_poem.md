# To Mom

In the space between our spirits,
Where understanding sometimes blurred,
I've watched you grow in wisdom,
As my own path has occurred.

Through meditation and mindfulness,
Tools I've learned to embrace,
I see you finding pieces of peace,
At your own gentle pace.

Remember when I urged you to travel?
To step beyond the known?
It wasn't just about distance,
But watching your soul grown.

We may have had our struggles,
Two strong souls learning to bend,
But in this spiritual journey,
I see our hearts start to mend.

You challenge me to be better,
In ways you may not know,
And as we both keep evolving,
I feel our bond still grow.

So here's to new beginnings,
To healing day by day,
To finding deeper connection,
As we both find our way.

*Written with love,
Johnathan*
