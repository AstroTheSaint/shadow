# Journal Structure Guide

## Daily Entry Template

Each daily entry should be named `YYYY-MM-DD.md` and include:

### Morning Reflection
- Current location and environment
- Dream recall (if any)
- Meditation insights
- Intention setting for the day
- Gratitude points (3 minimum)

### Evening Review
- Energy level tracking (1-10)
- Key events and experiences
- Lessons learned
- Technology usage reflection
- Connection quality (interactions with others)

### Spiritual Journey
- Divine moments noticed
- Faith challenges/victories
- Meditation experiences
- Signs and synchronicities

### Personal Growth
- Skills developed
- Books/content consumed
- Ideas and innovations
- Areas of resistance/growth

## Monthly Reviews
Create `YYYY-MM-review.md` files including:
- Progress on personal goals
- Pattern recognition
- Relationship development
- Location changes and travel impact
- Business and project evolution

## Quarterly Deep Dives
Create `YYYY-QX-reflection.md` files focusing on:
- Major shifts in perspective
- Evolution of spiritual practice
- Technology/humanity balance
- Global exploration insights
- Community building progress

## Special Entries

### Travel Journals
`travel/LOCATION-YYYY-MM.md`
- Cultural observations
- Personal transformations
- Divine appointments
- Local connections made
- Impact on worldview

### Project Insights
`projects/PROJECT-NAME-journal.md`
- Innovation breakthroughs
- Integration of spirituality and technology
- Community impact
- Learning moments
- Future visions

### Relationship Chronicles
`relationships/NAME-journey.md`
- Evolution of key relationships
- Lessons in connection
- Growth through interaction
- Shared spiritual moments
- Impact on personal development

## Journal Prompts

### Daily Reflection Questions
1. How did I balance technology and presence today?
2. What divine signs did I notice?
3. How did I serve others?
4. What triggered growth or resistance?
5. Where did I find joy in stillness?

### Weekly Integration
1. How aligned were my actions with my values?
2. What patterns emerged in my spiritual practice?
3. How did I balance global exploration with rootedness?
4. What relationships deepened or challenged me?
5. How did I integrate innovation with wisdom?

*Note: This is a living document. Adapt the structure as your journey evolves. Remember to date all entries and back them up regularly.*
