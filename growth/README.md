## Documentation Guidelines

1. **Regular Updates**
   - Weekly progress notes
   - Monthly reviews
   - Quarterly deep dives
   - Annual reflections

2. **Format Structure**
   - Date and context
   - Area of growth
   - Key insights
   - Action steps
   - Integration notes

3. **Cross-References**
   - Link to relevant journal entries
   - Connect to relationship impacts
   - Tie to professional outcomes
   - Note spiritual implications

*Remember: Growth is not linear. Document both progress and setbacks as part of the journey. Focus on integration over pure achievement.*
