# AI Shadow Agent Experiment

## Project Overview
Creating an AI agent that authentically replicates my social media presence and engagement patterns across platforms.

### Core Objective
To develop an AI version of myself that can:
- Engage authentically across social platforms
- Identify and interact with relevant content
- Maintain my voice and personality
- Scale my digital presence while preserving authenticity
